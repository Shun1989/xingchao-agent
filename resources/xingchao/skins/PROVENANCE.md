## Migration-only identity source

`../lanxi-key-art.png` is pre-existing project reference material used only to anchor Lanxi's face, hair, adult proportions, and painterly identity during captain generation. It was not copied, renamed, or presented as any of the ten fleet outputs. Its original provenance and license remain those of the source project; this Task 2 manifest does not relicense it.

## Deterministic raster operations

Captain base and uniform sources were deliberately generated on flat `#00FF00`, retained in the built-in generated-image store, and keyed with the controller-approved fixed filter `chromakey=0x00FF00:0.15:0.03,despill=green:mix=0.10`. They were proportionally contained and centered on 1600×2200 transparent canvases, then encoded as lossless WebP. No painted mask, subject reconstruction, or manual edge repair was used. Static fallbacks are color-correct composites of each accepted full-uniform captain over its accepted fleet backdrop.
